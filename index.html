Sacred Footsteps & Living Landscapes

Sri Lanka has been a place of pilgrimage for more than two thousand years. Walk beneath the Sri Maha Bodhi tree in Anuradhapura, climb the hill at Mihintale where, according to tradition, Buddhism first reached the island, and visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy. Between these holy places, lakes, tea hills and quiet coasts give you room to rest.

➔ Ask Our Experts
You can visit Sri Lanka year-round! As a tropical island, Sri Lanka enjoys abundant sunshine and warm temperatures throughout the year. The island experiences two gentle monsoon seasons: the southwest monsoon (May to September) makes the East and North coasts ideal, while the northeast monsoon (November to March) makes the West and South beaches sunny and dry.
Sri Lanka is one of the world's most welcoming and safe travel destinations. The nation is renowned for its hospitable people, tranquil Buddhist cultural traditions, and safe travel corridors. Lotus Ceylon provides government-licensed National Tourist Guide Lecturers, modern private air-conditioned touring vehicles, and direct coordinator support via WhatsApp, WeChat, and LINE for complete peace of mind.
Yes, most international travelers require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) prior to arrival. Your passport should have at least 6 months of validity from your arrival date. Visas can be easily applied online via the official government immigration portal (www.eta.gov.lk), and our team provides visa guidance to all our traveling guests.
Lightweight cotton or linen attire is recommended for the warm coastal and cultural regions. When traveling into the central Hill Country (Nuwara Eliya, Ella), pack light sweaters or cardigans as evening temperatures can dip to 12°C. When visiting sacred Buddhist or Hindu temples, shoulders and knees must be respectfully covered.
The Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) is the local currency. Major international credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, and Amex) are widely accepted at hotels, boutique restaurants, and shopping centers throughout the island. ATMs are readily accessible in all major towns.
Sri Lanka is an experiential touring destination where local road navigation, VIP ticket access to national parks, train reservations, and vetted boutique hotels require established local connections. Lotus Ceylon eliminates logistic stress, guarantees pre-reserved scenic train seats, and offers dedicated chauffeur guides.
