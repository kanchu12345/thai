Privacy Policy

Our sacred commitment to guest confidentiality, data security, and respectful communication.

Commitment to Privacy: Lotus Ceylon International Tours & Services strictly protects the confidentiality of your personal data. We never sell, lease, or monetize your information.

1. Information We Collect

To design your personalized travel itinerary and fulfill reservations, we collect necessary personal details, including:

  • Full names, nationalities, and passport details (as required for hotel check-ins and wildlife park entry permits).
  • Contact information, including email address, mobile phone number, and preferred messaging handles (WhatsApp, WeChat, or LINE).
  • Travel preferences, flight arrival/departure schedules, dietary requirements, and mobility considerations.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Design, quote, and execute your private travel itinerary.
  • Secure reservations with boutique hotels, tea estates, private safari jeeps, and scenic train tickets.
  • Coordinate seamless airport VIP meet-and-greet services upon arrival in Colombo.
  • Provide real-time concierge coordination and safety monitoring during your stay in Sri Lanka.

3. Data Security & Storage

All communications and guest records are stored in secure, encrypted cloud environments. Access is restricted strictly to authorized Lotus Ceylon travel designers and concierge personnel.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We only share essential traveler details with vetted operational partners strictly necessary for the execution of your trip (e.g., boutique hotels for guest check-in, Department of Wildlife Conservation for park permits, Sri Lanka Railways for train seat bookings). We never disclose guest information to third-party marketing companies.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections, or request complete deletion of your records from our systems following the completion of your journey. Inquiries may be addressed to: inquiry@lotusceylon.com.
