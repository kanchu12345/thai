Cookie Notice

Transparent information about our essential, privacy-first website cookies.

Privacy-First Browsing: Lotus Ceylon operates a clean, privacy-conscious website. We do not employ intrusive tracking pixels or sell user behavioral data.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser to ensure website functionality, remember language preferences, and deliver seamless navigation.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential Functional Cookies: Necessary to maintain session state, remember your selected language (English, Chinese, German, etc.), and process inquiry submissions.
  • Performance & Map Rendering: Used by our interactive Leaflet OpenStreetMap components to cache map tiles locally and deliver lightning-fast interactive routes.
  • Media Delivery: Allows Cloudinary and browser caches to load high-resolution imagery and WebP formats without redundant data usage.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can choose to disable or clear cookies at any time via your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may reset your selected language preference to default English on subsequent visits.
