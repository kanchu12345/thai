Explore Sri Lanka's
Sacred Sites & Famous Destinations

From the two-thousand-year-old sacred stupas of Anuradhapura and the golden temple of Kandy to mist-wrapped tea hills, wild leopard sanctuaries, and sun-drenched coastal forts. Discover every iconic destination across Ceylon.

Sacred Pilgrimage

Anuradhapura

Foundational Buddhist capital with towering white stupas (Ruwanwelisaya, Thuparamaya) and the sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi tree planted in 288 BC.

✦ Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi · Ruwanwelisaya · Jetavanaramaya

Sacred Pilgrimage

Mihintale

The venerated mountain sanctuary where Arahat Mahinda brought the message of Buddhism to King Devanampiyatissa in the 3rd century BC.

✦ Sacred Staircase · Ambhasthala Stupa · Aradhana Gala
View Related Journeys ➔
Sacred Pilgrimage

Kandy (Temple of the Tooth)

The hill capital honoring Sri Dalada Maligawa, enshrining the Sacred Tooth Relic of the Lord Buddha amidst royal lake waters and daily drum rituals.

✦ Sri Dalada Maligawa · Royal Botanical Gardens · Kandy Lake
View Related Journeys ➔
Sacred Pilgrimage

Dambulla

Magnificent UNESCO cave temple complex housing 153 Buddha statues and 2,100 square meters of sacred ceiling murals dating from the 1st century BC.

✦ 5 Sacred Caves · Golden Temple · Ancient Rock Murals
View Related Journeys ➔
Sacred Pilgrimage

Polonnaruwa

Medieval royal capital famed for the Gal Vihara, where four sublime Buddha images are masterfully carved from a single granite rock face.

✦ Gal Vihara · Vatadage · Parakrama Samudra
View Related Journeys ➔
Sacred Pilgrimage

Kelaniya

Revered riverside temple near Colombo, sanctified according to tradition by the Lord Buddha on his third visit to Sri Lanka.

✦ Kelaniya Raja Maha Vihara · Solias Mendis Murals · Duruthu Perahera
View Related Journeys ➔
Sacred Pilgrimage

Sri Pada (Adam's Peak)

The sacred 2,243m conical mountain crowned by the Sacred Footprint, climbed through night lantern trails for an unforgettable sunrise above the clouds.

✦ Sacred Footprint · Sunrise Shadow · Mountain Lantern Trail
View Related Journeys ➔
Sacred Pilgrimage

Kataragama

Sacred forest shrine complex venerated for millennia by Buddhists, Hindus, and indigenous Vedda communities beside the Menik Ganga.

✦ Maha Devale · Kiri Vehera · Menik Ganga River
View Related Journeys ➔
Cultural Heritage

Sigiriya Rock Citadel

The world-famous 'Lion Rock'—a 5th-century palace fortress towering 200m above the jungle, featuring the Mirror Wall and celestial maiden frescoes.

✦ Lion Paw Terrace · Mirror Wall · Water Gardens
