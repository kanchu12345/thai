## Realistic Driving Time & Distance Standards
All driving times and distances are calculated based on:
1. **Road Development Authority (RDA) Sri Lanka**: Official highway network classifications (E-class expressways, A-class arterial highways, B-class scenic secondary roads).
2. **Southern Expressway (E01) & Central Expressway (E04)**: Realistic average speeds of 80-100 km/h.
3. **Highland Winding Mountain Roads (A05 / A07)**: Realistic mountain transit speeds of 30-40 km/h due to steep gradients and hairpin bends.
4. **Chauffeur Comfort Standard**: All inter-city transfers include scheduled tea/refreshment stops and comfort breaks every 2 to 2.5 hours.

## Key Inter-City Transit Routes & Driving Estimates

### 1. Central Highlands & Tea Country
- **Colombo (CMB) to Kandy (via E04 / A01)**: ~115 km | 2.5 - 3.0 hrs
- **Kandy to Nuwara Eliya (via A05 scenic highland road)**: ~76 km | 2.5 - 3.0 hrs (winding mountain ascents past tea estates)
- **Nuwara Eliya to Ella (via A07 / B-roads)**: ~56 km | 1.5 - 2.0 hrs (or ~2.5 hrs on the scenic blue mountain train)
