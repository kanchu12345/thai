# Lotus Ceylon — Professional Photography Shot List

This shot list provides exact art direction, technical guidelines, and subject priorities for the commercial photography team.

## General Technical Standards
- **Resolution:** Minimum 2400px on the longest edge (raw files shot at 24MP+ preferred).
- **Orientation:** Primary landscape (16:9 or 3:2 ratio) for hero banners, cards, and lightbox galleries; supplementary vertical (4:5) for mobile hero viewports.
- **Color Grading:** Warm, natural, golden-hour tones. Avoid oversaturated HDR or artificial sky replacements. Showcase the authentic emerald lushness and golden light of Ceylon.
- **Model Diversity:** Sophisticated international couples, solo wellness seekers, multi-generational families. Wardrobe should be light linen, breathable cotton, culturally modest (shoulders and knees covered for sacred sites).

---

## Shot Specifications by Tour Category

### 1. Beach Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Couple on a secluded golden beach at Tangalle or Mirissa with coconut palms hanging over the shore.
- **Time of Day:** Golden Hour (06:00 – 06:45 AM or 17:30 – 18:15 PM).
- **In-Action Details:** Traditional stilt fishermen silhouetted against a crimson sunset in Koggala; Kosgoda baby sea turtle crawling toward moonlit surf; luxury beachfront villa private plunge pool.

### 2. Cultural & Heritage Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Buddhist monks walking past ancient granite pillars of Polonnaruwa Vatadage or Anuradhapura Ruwanwelisaya stupa in early morning golden light.
- **Time of Day:** 06:30 – 08:00 AM (soft morning mist and low sun angle).
- **In-Action Details:** Traditional oil lamp lighting ceremony at Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Kandy); sweeping aerial drone view of Sigiriya Lion Rock reflected in ancient water gardens; hands carving traditional wood or weaving lotus silk.

### 3. Wildlife & Nature Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Sri Lankan Leopard (*Panthera pardus kotiya*) resting on a sun-drenched granite boulder in Yala or Wilpattu.
- **Time of Day:** 06:00 – 07:30 AM or 15:30 – 17:30 PM.
- **In-Action Details:** Mother and baby Asian elephant touching trunks in Minneriya grass plains; Blue whale fluke breaching calm turquoise waters off Mirissa; luxury open-top safari jeep parked along dusty park tracks with binoculars and cold beverage setup.

### 4. Hill Country Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Iconic blue train crossing the Nine Arches Bridge in Demodara/Ella, framed by dense tea plantations and cloud forest.
- **Time of Day:** 07:00 – 08:30 AM (morning mountain light clearing mist).
- **In-Action Details:** Tea plucker in traditional saree harvesting "two leaves and a bud"; colonial stone fireplace and afternoon high tea spread at a luxury Ceylon tea planter's bungalow; couple admiring sweeping Ella Gap views.

### 5. Ayurvedic & Wellness Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Shirodhara treatment (warm herbal oil gently poured onto forehead) in an open-air pavilion surrounded by lotus ponds and bamboo groves.
- **Time of Day:** Soft diffused morning light or twilight with candle lanterns.
- **In-Action Details:** Ayurvedic doctor taking pulse (Nadi Pariksha) during a consultation; fresh herbal decoctions in ceramic bowls; guest meditating on a wooden yoga deck overlooking mist-shrouded rainforest.

### 6. Honeymoon & Romance Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Private candlelit dining pavilion set on a private sandbar with lanterns glowing and champagne chilling.
- **Time of Day:** Blue hour / dusk (18:15 – 19:15 PM).
- **In-Action Details:** Couple enjoying a private infinity pool overlooking the Indian Ocean; sunset catamaran sailing cruise off the coast of Weligama; flower-strewn luxury suite bed with chilled coconut water.

### 7. Family Discovery Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Parents and children smiling while observing wild elephants safely from a private safari vehicle in Udawalawe.
- **Time of Day:** Late afternoon (16:00 – 17:30 PM).
- **In-Action Details:** Family walking hand-in-hand along the ramparts of Galle Dutch Fort; children learning mask carving from a master artisan in Ambalangoda; family enjoying fresh king coconuts on a sunny plantation.

### 8. Adventure & Active Expeditions
- **Hero Scene:** Six-person raft navigating white-water rapids on the Kelani River in Kitulgala with rainforest spray.
- **Time of Day:** Bright mid-morning (09:30 – 11:00 AM).
- **In-Action Details:** Colorful kite surfer catching air over Kalpitiya lagoon; hikers reaching the precipice of World's End in Horton Plains; surfing clean morning waves at Arugam Bay.

### 9. Ramayana Heritage Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Devotional view of Seetha Amman Temple in Seetha Eliya, nestled against Hakgala rock cliffs.
- **Time of Day:** Early morning pujah (07:30 – 09:00 AM).
- **In-Action Details:** Munneswaram Shiva Temple brass oil lamps burning during evening aarti; Ravana Ella waterfall cascading down rocky crags; offering lotus flowers at sacred shrines.

### 10. Golf Tours
- **Hero Scene:** Golfer in mid-swing on the fairway of Nuwara Eliya Golf Club with colonial club house in the background and pine-clad hills.
- **Time of Day:** 07:00 – 08:30 AM (crisp mountain morning light).
- **In-Action Details:** Flagstick fluttering on the green with Dew-dusted turf; golfer teeing off alongside the Indian Ocean at Shangri-La Hambantota Golf Resort.
