Growing a Greener Future: Volunteer Reforestation in the Sinharaja Canopy

By Lotus Ceylon Conservation Desk

As Sri Lanka's last viable area of primary tropical rainforest, the Sinharaja Forest Reserve is a UNESCO World Heritage biodiversity hotspot of global significance. More than 60% of the trees are endemic, and many are considered critically endangered. In the canopy, over 50% of Sri Lanka's endemic wildlife species make their home.

The Buffer Zone Protection Initiative

Through our responsible travel charter, Lotus Ceylon contributes directly to native sapling nurseries along the southern buffer border. Guests participating in our Untamed Biosphere expeditions have the privileged opportunity to plant indigenous mahogany, ironwood (Na), and dipterocarp seedlings alongside community forestry wardens.

Silent Walks Through Living Evolution

Trekking under Sinharaja's dense emerald canopy is a transformative sensory experience. Giant tree ferns, rare carnivorous pitcher plants, and colorful mixed-species bird feeding flocks create a symphony of natural wonder that has remained undisturbed for millennia.
