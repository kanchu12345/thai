# LOTUS CEYLON — CLIENT CMS GUIDE
**Welcome to your Lotus Ceylon Visual Website Editor**

Your website admin panel is designed to be **simple, visual, and intuitive**. You don't need any technical or programming knowledge. You can edit any text, image, video, button, or link simply by clicking it directly on the page!

---

## 1. How to Open the Visual Editor
1. Log into your Admin Panel (`admin/index.html`).
2. In the left sidebar under **Website Pages**, click **🏠 Home Page** (or any other page).
3. The editor opens with a **live preview of your real website** on the left, and your **editing tools** on the right.

---

## 2. How to Edit Any Text or Heading
1. Move your mouse over any text on the website preview. You will see a golden dashed outline.
2. **Click the text**.
3. The right-hand panel immediately opens the editor for that item.
4. Type your new text in the box. **You will see the text change on the website instantly!**
5. Click **💾 Save Draft** to save your work, or **🚀 Publish Live** to push it to the live website immediately.

---

## 3. How to Change the Hero Banner (Photo or Video)
1. On the right panel, click **🎯 Hero Banner** under *Quick Jump*.
2. You can choose whether you want a **📷 Photo Image** or a **🎥 Hero Video**:
   - **For Photo**: Click **☁️ Upload** to upload a new high-resolution photo from your computer, or choose from your Media Library.
   - **For Video**: Paste a video URL (MP4, Cloudinary, or YouTube link). Autoplay and loop are enabled automatically.
3. Use the **Dark Overlay Slider** to make the background darker or lighter so that your headline text remains crystal clear.
4. Update the **Headline**, **Subheading**, and **Button Text** directly.

---

## 4. How to Change Photos and Images
1. Click on any photo on the page.
2. The right panel will show the photo preview with two options:
   - **📁 Browse**: Pick from existing uploaded photos in your media library.
   - **☁️ Upload**: Select a new photo directly from your computer or phone.
3. Enter a short description in the **Image Alt Text** box (helpful for Google search rankings).

---

## 5. How to Edit Translations (Multi-Language)
1. At the top of the editor, click the **Language** dropdown (e.g. 🇫🇷 French, 🇩🇪 German, 🇨🇳 Chinese, etc.).
2. The entire website preview will switch to that language.
3. Click any text you want to translate.
4. Click the **✨ Auto-Translate from EN** button to instantly generate a translation from your English text, or type your own custom translation!

---

## 6. How to Switch Between Desktop and Mobile Phone View
1. In the top bar, click **📱 Mobile**.
2. The preview instantly transforms into an authentic **iPhone screen (390px)** so you can verify exactly how your website looks to mobile visitors.
3. Click **🖥️ Desktop** to switch back to the full widescreen view.

---

## 7. How to Add Tour Packages & Guest Reviews
- **Tour Packages**: Click **Tour Packages** in the left sidebar to add, duplicate, edit prices, routes, itineraries, or photos.
- **Guest Reviews**: Click **Guest Reviews** in the left sidebar to add new client testimonials and star ratings.

---

## 8. Publishing Your Changes Live
- Whenever you make changes, the status indicator in the top bar turns **🟠 Unsaved changes**.
- When you are satisfied with your edits, click the gold **🚀 Publish Live** button at the top right.
- Your updates are immediately active on your live website for travelers around the world!
