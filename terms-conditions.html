Clear, transparent, and fair booking conditions for our discerning global guests.

1. Introduction & Booking Confirmation

Lotus Ceylon International Tours & Services (Private) Limited (hereafter referred to as "Lotus Ceylon", "we", "us", or "our") specializes in bespoke, private inbound journeys across Sri Lanka. A booking is confirmed when your customized itinerary is mutually agreed upon in writing, and the requisite deposit has been received.

2. 100% Tailor-Made Private Journeys

Every itinerary designed by Lotus Ceylon is private. You will not be merged with third-party tour groups or shared coach departures. Your designated air-conditioned private vehicle and government-licensed chauffeur guide are exclusively reserved for your traveling party.

3. Pricing & Currency

All tour quotations are clearly stated in US Dollars (USD) or your preferred currency as confirmed in writing. Quotations include all private road transportation, certified chauffeur-guide services, curated accommodation as specified, and entry permits as listed in your itinerary. International airfares and personal gratuities are excluded unless explicitly noted.

4. Traveler Health, Insurance & Documentation

All travelers are responsible for obtaining comprehensive personal travel insurance covering medical expenses, emergency repatriation, trip cancellation, and baggage loss. All travelers must hold a passport with at least six months of validity from the date of entry and obtain a valid Sri Lankan Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA / eVisa) prior to departure.

Lotus Ceylon shall not be liable for changes, delays, or cancellations caused by events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to severe weather anomalies, natural disasters, national strikes, government travel restrictions, or airport closures.
