ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 1

Arrival in Negombo • Coastal Sanctuary

Warm arrival reception by your Lotus Ceylon private chauffeur guide. Transfer to your luxury Negombo boutique stay.

🏨 Overnight: Negombo Beach Boutique Resort 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 2

Wilpattu Sacred Monasteries & Anuradhapura

Drive northward toward Anuradhapura, visiting tranquil forest hermitages along the border of Wilpattu. Check into your heritage sanctuary.

🏨 Overnight: Anuradhapura Heritage Sanctuary 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 3

The First Kingdom: Sacred Anuradhapura & Mihintale

Full day dedicated to the sacred stupas of Ruwanwelisaya, Abhayagiri, and the sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi. Sunset ascent of Mihintale.

🏨 Overnight: Anuradhapura Heritage Sanctuary 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 4

Journey into the Deep North • Jaffna Peninsula

Cross the Elephant Pass causeway into the vibrant northern cultural capital of Jaffna. Visit the monumental Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil.

🏨 Overnight: Jaffna Luxury Heritage Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 5

Jaffna Dutch Fort & Nagadeepa Sacred Island

Explore the star-shaped Jaffna Dutch Fort and take a wooden ferry to sacred Nagadeepa Purana Viharaya and Nagapooshani Amman Temple.

🏨 Overnight: Jaffna Luxury Heritage Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 6

Southward to the Cultural Triangle • Sigiriya

Descend southward to Sigiriya. Relax in luxury eco-villas surrounded by peaceful nature and paddy fields.

🏨 Overnight: Sigiriya Luxury Water Pavilion 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 7

Sigiriya Dawn Sky Citadel & Rural Village Life

Early morning climb of Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress. Afternoon village catamaran ride and authentic clay pot meal in Hiriwadunna.

🏨 Overnight: Sigiriya Luxury Water Pavilion 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 8

Polonnaruwa Medieval Capital Ruins by Bicycle

Cycle among the ancient stone wonders of Polonnaruwa, marveling at Gal Vihara and the lotus-shaped baths.

🏨 Overnight: Sigiriya Luxury Water Pavilion 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 9

Dambulla Cave Temples to Royal Hill Capital Kandy

Explore the five painted caves of Dambulla, then journey into the misty mountains of Kandy. Attend the evening Tooth Relic puja.

🏨 Overnight: Kandy Royal Heritage Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 10

Peradeniya Botanic Gardens & Three Ancient Temples

Discover the 14th-century Gadaladeniya, Embekke (famous for intricate wood carvings), and Lankatilaka temples, followed by the Royal Botanic Gardens.

🏨 Overnight: Kandy Royal Heritage Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 11

Colonial Highlands of Nuwara Eliya & Tea Estates

Wind through emerald tea-carpeted mountains to 'Little England'. Tour a heritage tea factory and sip pure Ceylon single-estate tea.

🏨 Overnight: Nuwara Eliya Heritage Tea Bungalow 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 12

Scenic Mountain Descent to UNESCO Galle Dutch Fort

Descend through tea valleys and cascading waterfalls to the historic southern coastline and Galle Dutch Fort.

🏨 Overnight: Galle Fort Luxury Heritage Suite 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 13

Ramparts, Bastions & Living Maritime History of Galle

Full day soaking in the ambiance of Galle Fort: visit the Dutch Reformed Church, maritime museum, and enjoy sunset drinks along Flag Rock Bastion.

🏨 Overnight: Galle Fort Luxury Heritage Suite 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
Day 14

Colombo Metropolitan Heritage Drive

Travel to Colombo along the southern expressway. Tour the historic Pettah markets, Old Parliament, and National Museum before your farewell dinner.

🏨 Overnight: Colombo 5★ City Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner div
Day 15

Departure from Sri Lanka

Bid farewell to Ceylon as your private chauffeur guide escorts you to the airport for your return flight.

🏨 Overnight: Onward Journey / Return Flight 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗
