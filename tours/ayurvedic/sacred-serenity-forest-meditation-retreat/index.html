A journey through Sacred Serenity & Forest Meditation Retreat

Arrival at CMB airport. Private transfer to a serene forest hermitage retreat near Dambulla surrounded by birdsong.

Overnight: Forest Meditation Sanctuary, Dambulla Meals: Dinner Included

Morning silent walk through the five sacred cave sanctuaries of Dambulla. Introduction to mindfulness breathing (Anapanasati).

Overnight: Forest Meditation Sanctuary, Dambulla Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Visit an active Buddhist forest monastery (Aranya Senasanaya). Experience seated meditation under ancient canopy trees.

Overnight: Forest Meditation Sanctuary, Dambulla Meals: Breakfast & Vegetarian Lunch

Explore the stone monastic bridges and meditation platforms of 1st-century BCE Ritigala monastery secluded deep in nature.

Overnight: Forest Meditation Sanctuary, Dambulla Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Drive into the hills of Kandy. Participate in evening oil lamp offering and meditative drumming puja at the Temple of the Tooth.

Overnight: Hillside Heritage Sanctuary, Kandy Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Visit the 14th-century Gadaladeniya and Embekke temples. Mindful walking along historic rural village pathways.

Overnight: Hillside Heritage Sanctuary, Kandy Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Drive down to your peaceful oceanfront Ayurvedic hospital-retreat in Bentota. Settle into total tranquility.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Dinner

Comprehensive doctor assessment. Begin tailored daily detox therapies, warm herbal oil massage, and herbal steam baths.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Full Board

Deep restorative Shirodhara oil flow over the forehead complemented by Tibetan singing bowl acoustic frequency balancing.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Full Board

Facial herbal treatments using sandalwood and saffron masks. Daily yoga, pranayama breathing, and peaceful ocean reflection.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Full Board

Medicinal poultice therapies for joint mobility. Walking meditation in lush herb gardens smelling medicinal cinnamon leaves.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Full Board

Gentle detoxification day supported by physician-prescribed herbal decoctions and quiet contemplative reading.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Full Board

Final medical consultation. Traditional blessing cord ceremony by Buddhist monks wishing health, peace, and longevity.

Overnight: Oceanfront Ayurvedic Sanctuary, Bentota Meals: Ayurvedic Full Board

Private chauffeur transfer to CMB airport, completely revitalized in body, clarity, and spirit.
