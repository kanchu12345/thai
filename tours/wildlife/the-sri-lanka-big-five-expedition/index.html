ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 1

Arrival in Negombo • Coastline Gateway

Receive an authentic VIP welcome by your Lotus Ceylon safari host. Transfer to your boutique seaside resort in Negombo to relax before your expedition.

🏨 Overnight: Negombo Beach Boutique Resort 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 2

Wilpattu National Park • Tracking the Sloth Bear & Leopard

Drive northward to Wilpattu National Park. Afternoon 4x4 safari traversing sand trails and scenic villus to spot sloth bears and leopards.

🏨 Overnight: Wilpattu Luxury Safari Camp 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 3

Full-Day Wilpattu Deep Wilderness Drive

Spend an entire day immersed in Wilpattu’s untouched wilderness with packed bush breakfast and lunch under shaded mahogany trees.

🏨 Overnight: Wilpattu Luxury Safari Camp 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 4

Transit to Habarana • Minneriya Elephant Gathering

Drive to Habarana. In the late afternoon, embark on an open 4x4 safari at Minneriya or Kaudulla to witness hundreds of wild elephants.

🏨 Overnight: Habarana Eco-Luxury Jungle Resort 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 5

Minneriya / Hurulu Eco-Park Safari & Night Jungle Walk

Morning at leisure at your jungle resort. Afternoon safari in Hurulu Eco Park. Evening guided night walk to spot the rare Grey Slender Loris.

🏨 Overnight: Habarana Eco-Luxury Jungle Resort 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 6

Scenic Route to Udawalawe National Park

Travel southward toward the dry-zone plains of Udawalawe, home to dense resident herds of wild elephants.

🏨 Overnight: Udawalawe Safari Lodge 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 7

Elephant Transit Home & Udawalawe Reservoir Safari

Visit the Born Free-supported Elephant Transit Home during morning feeding. Afternoon 4x4 safari across Udawalawe’s open grasslands.

🏨 Overnight: Udawalawe Safari Lodge 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 8

Transit to Yala National Park

Drive to Yala on the southeast coast. Check into your luxury glamping camp. Sunset game drive across Yala’s coastal scrublands.

🏨 Overnight: Yala Luxury Safari Tented Camp 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 9

Yala Block 1 & Block 5 Leopard Safaris

Morning and afternoon game drives in Yala tracking leopards, jackals, sambar deer, and marsh crocodiles.

🏨 Overnight: Yala Luxury Safari Tented Camp 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 10

Coastal Route to Mirissa Whale Sanctuary

Drive along the southern coastline to Mirissa. Relax on golden beaches and enjoy fresh seafood dining under coconut palms.

🏨 Overnight: Mirissa Beachfront Boutique Villa 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 11

Blue Whale & Sperm Whale Oceanic Safari

Private sunrise boat charter into the deep submarine canyon off Mirissa, tracking blue whales, sperm whales, and leaping dolphin super-pods.

🏨 Overnight: Mirissa Beachfront Boutique Villa 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 12

UNESCO Galle Fort Ramparts & Coastal Relaxation

Leisurely morning followed by an afternoon walking tour of historic Galle Dutch Fort and sunset drinks overlooking the Indian Ocean.

🏨 Overnight: Galle Luxury Boutique Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 13

Return to Colombo • Farewell Dinner

Drive along the southern highway to Colombo. Evening celebration dinner with your private guide.

🏨 Overnight: Colombo 5★ City Hotel 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 14

Departure from Sri Lanka

Transfer to the international airport for your homeward journey with extraordinary wildlife memories.

🏨 Overnight: Departure 🍽️ Breakfast & Gourmet Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
