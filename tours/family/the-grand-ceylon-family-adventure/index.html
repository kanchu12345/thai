ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 01

Welcome to Ceylon & Coastal Relaxation in Negombo

Arrival at CMB airport. Private transfer to a family resort with swimming pool and lush tropical gardens.

🏨 Overnight: Coastal Family Resort, Negombo 🍽️ Dinner Included 🚗 Approx. 45 Mins - 1 Hour (35 km)
Day 02

Drive North to Wilpattu National Park Border

Journey through scenic coconut estates to the borders of Wilpattu, Sri Lanka's largest and oldest national park.

🏨 Overnight: Safari Forest Resort, Wilpattu 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 03

Wilpattu Safari & Sandstone Ruins of Anuradhapura

Morning jeep safari around natural sand-rimmed water basins (Villus). Afternoon visit to the ancient sacred city of Anuradhapura.

🏨 Overnight: Heritage Resort, Anuradhapura 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 04

Sacred Bodhi Tree & Transfer to Sigiriya

Visit the 2,300-year-old Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi tree. Drive south to a beautiful resort in Sigiriya with outdoor pool.

🏨 Overnight: Family Resort, Sigiriya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 05

Sigiriya Rock Palace & Chena Farm Village Lunch

Morning ascent of Sigiriya Rock Fortress. Enjoy an authentic rural lunch cooked in clay pots over coconut husks.

🏨 Overnight: Family Resort, Sigiriya 🍽️ Breakfast, Farm Lunch & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 06

Dambulla Golden Caves & Matale Spice Gardens

Visit the majestic painted cave temple of Dambulla. Touch fresh cinnamon and cocoa pods in Matale spice groves.

🏨 Overnight: Lakeside Resort, Kandy 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 07

Kandy Sacred Temple & Royal Botanic Gardens

Explore the Temple of the Tooth and stroll through the immense bamboo forests of Peradeniya Botanic Gardens.

🏨 Overnight: Lakeside Resort, Kandy 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 08

Scenic Mountain Train Ride to Nuwara Eliya

Board the famous hill country train. Kids delight in mountain tunnels and cascading waterfalls visible from windows.

🏨 Overnight: Historic Mountain Resort, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 09

Pedro Tea Plantation & Gregory Lake Family Fun

Learn how tea is made from leaf to cup. Afternoon pedal boating and cycling along Lake Gregory promenade.

🏨 Overnight: Historic Mountain Resort, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 10

Descent Past Waterfalls to Bentota Golden Coast

Scenic drive winding past Devon and St. Clair's waterfalls, descending to the warm golden beaches of Bentota.

🏨 Overnight: Beachfront Luxury Resort, Bentota 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 11

Madu River Mangrove Boat Safari & Fish Therapy

Speedboat safari through 64 mangrove islands. Visit a cinnamon-peeling island and watch birds in their natural sanctuary.

🏨 Overnight: Beachfront Luxury Resort, Bentota 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 12

Kosgoda Sea Turtle Hatchery & Baby Release

Hands-on conservation experience learning about sea turtles and releasing newly-hatched baby turtles into the sea.

🏨 Overnight: Beachfront Luxury Resort, Bentota 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 13

Bentota Water Sports & Farewell Family Beach Dinner

Exciting banana boat and tube rides on Bentota lagoon. In the evening, gather for a special private farewell seafood beach dinner.

🏨 Overnight: Beachfront Luxury Resort, Bentota 🍽️ Breakfast & Special Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 14

Express Highway Transfer to Colombo Airport & Departure

After breakfast, enjoy a smooth private highway transfer to CMB airport for your flight home.

🏨 Overnight: Departure 🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
