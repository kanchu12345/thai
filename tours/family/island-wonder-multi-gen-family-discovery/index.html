Island Wonder Multi-Gen Family Discovery

09 Nights and 10 Days

Safe, gentle, and deeply engaging exploration for grandparents, parents, and children alike.

A journey through Island Wonder Multi-Gen Family Discovery

Meet your family-friendly chauffeur guide at CMB airport. Short 20-minute drive to Negombo beach resort with warm swimming pools.

Overnight: Family Beach Resort, Negombo Meals: Dinner Included

Watch majestic elephant herds bathing in Ma Oya river. Drive into Cultural Triangle to a spacious eco-resort with gardens.

Overnight: Family Eco-Resort, Sigiriya Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Explore the ancient water gardens at the base of Sigiriya rock (easy walking for all generations). Traditional catamaran ride.

Overnight: Family Eco-Resort, Sigiriya Meals: Breakfast, Village Lunch & Dinner

Afternoon open-top jeep safari into Minneriya National Park to observe large herds of wild elephants with playful calves.

Overnight: Family Eco-Resort, Sigiriya Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Visit an aromatic spice garden where kids touch cinnamon bark and vanilla pods. Evening traditional cultural dance show in Kandy.

Overnight: Family Hillside Resort, Kandy Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Walk among giant Javanese fig trees and vibrant orchids. Board the scenic train for a gentle ride into tea hills.

Overnight: Colonial Mountain Resort, Nuwara Eliya Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Kids pick tea leaves with mini baskets. Afternoon pedal-boating and pony rides along the scenic banks of Lake Gregory.

Overnight: Colonial Mountain Resort, Nuwara Eliya Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Scenic highway drive to the golden shores of Bentota. Relax by the ocean and enjoy calm swimming waters.

Overnight: Luxury Family Beach Resort, Bentota Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Visit the sea turtle conservation centre. Children hold baby turtles and release healthy hatchlings directly into the ocean at dusk.

Overnight: Luxury Family Beach Resort, Bentota Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Enjoy a final family swim and tropical fruit breakfast before private transfer to CMB airport for your flight home.

Overnight: Departure Meals: Breakfast Included
