ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 01

Arrival in Colombo & Transfer to Kelani River Valley

Meet your private chauffeur guide and drive into the rainforest valley of Kitulgala, backdrop to 'The Bridge on the River Kwai'.

🏨 Overnight: Riverside Eco-Luxury Lodge, Kitulgala 🍽️ Dinner Included 🚗 Approx. 45 Mins - 1 Hour (35 km)
Day 02

Rainforest Waterfalls & Secret Canyon Pools

Morning trek through riverine rainforest to hidden waterfalls. Enjoy a refreshing natural rock pool dip surrounded by emerald forest.

🏨 Overnight: Riverside Eco-Luxury Lodge, Kitulgala 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 03

Aberdeen & Laxapana Twin Cataracts Route

Scenic drive visiting Aberdeen Falls and the roaring 126-meter Laxapana cataract. Ascend to Nuwara Eliya.

🏨 Overnight: Boutique Alpine Retreat, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 04

St. Clair’s & Devon Falls Panoramic Circuit

Visit 'Little Niagara of Sri Lanka' St. Clair's Falls. Taste artisanal Ceylon teas overlooking rolling tea valleys.

🏨 Overnight: Boutique Alpine Retreat, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 05

Horton Plains Wilderness & World’s End

Hike the 9.5 km loop of Horton Plains. Marvel at misty Baker's Falls cascading over dark volcanic stone.

🏨 Overnight: Boutique Alpine Retreat, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 06

Transfer to Misty Haputale & High Tea

Drive along cliff-hugging mountain roads to Haputale, perched on the razor-edge of the southern highland ridge.

🏨 Overnight: Ridge-Top Boutique Villa, Haputale 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 07

Lipton’s Seat Sunrise & Dambatenne Tea Estate

Dawn drive to Sir Thomas Lipton’s favorite vantage point. Gaze over 5 provinces bathed in morning golden light.

🏨 Overnight: Ridge-Top Boutique Villa, Haputale 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 08

Bambarakanda & Diyaluma Waterfall Adventures

Stand beneath Sri Lanka's tallest waterfall, Bambarakanda (263m), and trek to the natural infinity pools atop Diyaluma Falls.

🏨 Overnight: Luxury Mountain Villa, Ella 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 09

Ella Gap & Nine Arches Bridge Walk

Explore the vibrant cafes, spice gardens, and historic railway viaduct of picturesque Ella valley.

🏨 Overnight: Luxury Mountain Villa, Ella 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 10

Adam’s Peak Wilderness Foothills / Sri Pada Trail

Transfer to the sacred foothills of Adam's Peak. Prepare for the evening pilgrimage trail or relax in cloud-forest nature.

🏨 Overnight: Pilgrimage Mountain Resort, Nallathanniya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 11

Sacred Sunrise Over Adam’s Peak Summit

Experience the shadow of the sacred peak projected onto the morning cloud blanket. Return for a well-deserved rest.

🏨 Overnight: Pilgrimage Mountain Resort, Nallathanniya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 12

Descent to Colombo & Departure

Enjoy a leisurely morning breakfast before private chauffeur transfer to Colombo or CMB airport.

🏨 Overnight: Departure 🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
