ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 01

Arrival in Colombo & Coastal Journey to Chilaw

Meet your private scholarly guide. Drive north to Chilaw. Evening puja at Munneswaram and Manavari temples.

🏨 Overnight: Coastal Hotel, Chilaw/Kalpitiya 🍽️ Vegetarian Dinner 🚗 Approx. 45 Mins - 1 Hour (35 km)
Day 02

Mannar Island, Thiruketheeswaram & Rama Setu (Adam's Bridge)

Drive across Mannar causeway. Visit ancient Thiruketheeswaram Shiva temple. Stand at the sandbar vantage point of Rama Setu.

🏨 Overnight: Heritage Lodge, Mannar/Jaffna 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 03

Naguleswaram Temple & Jaffna Cultural Heritage

Visit Naguleswaram temple and sacred Keerimalai springs where sage Nagula Muni bathed. Tour historic Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil.

🏨 Overnight: Heritage Hotel, Jaffna 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 04

Trincomalee Koneswaram Temple & Kanniya Hot Springs

Cross to the east coast. Visit Koneswaram Temple perched atop Swami Rock cliff face. Visit the 7 sacred Kanniya hot wells.

🏨 Overnight: Beach Resort, Trincomalee 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 05

Ascent to Hill Capital of Kandy

Scenic drive into central highlands. Evening cultural drum offering and blessings at the sacred Temple of the Tooth.

🏨 Overnight: Hillside Hotel, Kandy 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 06

Sri Bhakta Hanuman Temple & Seetha Amman Temple

Visit the 16ft Hanuman statue in Ramboda. Continue to Seetha Eliya to venerate Seetha Amman Temple and see Hanuman footprints.

🏨 Overnight: Colonial Resort, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 07

Ashoka Vatika (Hakgala) & Divurumpola Agni Pariksha

Walk through Hakgala Botanical Gardens (Ashoka Vatika). Visit Divurumpola Temple where Sita Devi underwent the trial of fire.

🏨 Overnight: Colonial Resort, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 08

Ravana Falls, Ravana Cave & Ella Mountain Valley

Explore the cascading Ravana Falls and trek to Ravana Cave where King Ravana hid Sita Devi. Enjoy breathtaking Ella Gap views.

🏨 Overnight: Mountain Villa, Ella 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 09

Kelaniya Temple & Colombo Capital Pilgrimage

Scenic highway drive to Colombo. Visit Kelaniya Raja Maha Vihara and Sri Anjaneyar Temple in Dehiwala.

🏨 Overnight: Luxury Hotel, Colombo 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗
Day 10

Final Blessings & Departure Transfer

Conclude your comprehensive sacred pilgrimage with private chauffeur transfer to CMB airport.

🏨 Overnight: Onward Journey / Return Flight 🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗
