ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 01

Arrival & Coastal Transition in Negombo

Meet your private adventure guide. Rest and refresh overlooking the ocean.

🍽️ Meals: Dinner Included 🏨 Stay: Beach Villa, Negombo
Day 02

Into the Deep UNESCO Sinharaja Rainforest

Drive to the borders of Sinharaja. Trek under towering rainforest canopy with leech socks and certified biodiversity tracker.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Rainforest Canopy Lodge, Sinharaja
Day 03

Sinharaja Bird Waves & Hidden Waterfall Rappel

Witness the world's largest mixed-species bird feeding flocks. Enjoy controlled waterfall abseiling in dense jungle.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Rainforest Canopy Lodge, Sinharaja
Day 04

Ascent to Ella High Ridges

Drive into the central highlands. Hike through tea terraces to Little Adam’s Peak and explore Ravana’s Cave.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Mountain Lodge, Ella
Day 05

Descent to Arugam Bay Surf Mecca

Cross the dramatic transition from misty tea highlands to arid eastern coastline and world-renowned surf bays.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Beachfront Surf Sanctuary, Arugam Bay
Day 06

Dawn Surf Session & Lagoon Wildlife Kayak

Catch golden dawn waves at Main Point. Afternoon kayak safari through Pottuvil lagoon watching wild elephants and crocodiles.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Beachfront Surf Sanctuary, Arugam Bay
Day 07

Secret Point Surfing & Kudumbigala Monastery Trek

Trek through scrub jungles to ancient rock monastery of Kudumbigala with sweeping 360-degree views across untamed wilderness.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Beachfront Surf Sanctuary, Arugam Bay
Day 08

Kumana (Yala East) National Park Off-Road Safari

Full-day 4WD expedition in Kumana tracking elusive coastal leopards, sloth bears, and massive aquatic bird breeding colonies.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Safari Lunch & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Tented Safari Camp, Kumana Border
Day 09

Southern Coastline Transition to Galle Ramparts

Drive along scenic southern shoreline. Evening craft beer walk along historic cobblestone Dutch streets of Galle Fort.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Historic Fort Hotel, Galle
Day 10

Ocean Jet-Ski & Mangrove Lagoon Jet Exploration

Morning coastal watersports followed by relaxed heritage walking tour and sunset cocktails on the lighthouse ramparts.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Stay: Historic Fort Hotel, Galle
Day 11

Express Highway Transfer & Departure

Private chauffeur transfer via southern expressway directly to CMB airport.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast Included 🏨 Stay: Departure
