ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 01

Arrival in Colombo & Direct Expedition Transfer to Kitulgala

Meet your adventure chauffeur guide. Drive to Kitulgala river canyon basecamp. Safety briefing and gear fitting.

🏨 Overnight: Adventure Safari Camp, Kitulgala 🍽️ Dinner Included 🚗 Approx. 45 Mins - 1 Hour (35 km)
Day 02

White Water Rafting & Confidence Waterfall Jump

Conquer Head Chopper, Virgin's Breast, and Butter Crunch Grade III/IV rapids. Afternoon river canyoning and natural rock sliding.

🏨 Overnight: Adventure Safari Camp, Kitulgala 🍽️ Breakfast, BBQ Lunch & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 03

Ascent to Knuckles UNESCO Wilderness Range

Transfer to the rugged Knuckles mountain massif. Trek past isolated cardamom villages and crystal mountain streams.

🏨 Overnight: Wilderness Eco Lodge, Knuckles 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 04

Knuckles Ridge Cloud Forest Summit Trek

Full day 16km trek across mist-shrouded knife-edge ridges and endemic pygmy forests with certified naturalists.

🏨 Overnight: Wilderness Eco Lodge, Knuckles 🍽️ Breakfast, Trail Lunch & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 05

Transfer to Ella & Flying Ravana Mega Zip-Line

Scenic mountain drive to Ella. Strap in for Sri Lanka’s fastest mega zip-line flying at 80 km/h over tea valleys.

🏨 Overnight: Mountain Action Lodge, Ella 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 06

Ella Rock Scramble & Descent to South Coast Surf

Morning off-trail rock scramble up Ella Rock. Afternoon highway transfer to Weligama Bay surf sanctuary.

🏨 Overnight: Boutique Surf Villa, Mirissa/Weligama 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 07

Private Surf Coaching & Sunset Ocean Cruise

Private morning surf session tailored to your skill level. Afternoon coastal speedboat cruise searching for marine life.

🏨 Overnight: Boutique Surf Villa, Mirissa/Weligama 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 08

Express Highway Transfer to Colombo & Departure

Final morning beach dip followed by private highway transfer to CMB airport for onward journey.

🏨 Overnight: Departure 🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
