ITINERARY

Detailed Day-by-Day Journey

Every element of this bespoke itinerary is privately chauffeured, seamlessly managed, and fully customizable to your personal travel pace and desires.

Day 01

VIP Airport Arrival & The Kingsbury Colombo Check-In

VIP expedited arrival at CMB airport. Check into your ocean-facing luxury suite. Unpack and inspect golf gear.

🏨 Overnight: The Kingsbury 5-Star Hotel, Colombo 🍽️ Dinner & Welcome Cocktails 🚗 Approx. 45 Mins - 1 Hour (35 km)
Day 02

Round 1: 18 Holes at Royal Colombo Golf Club

Morning 18-hole championship round on historic colonial parkland fairways.

🏨 Overnight: The Kingsbury, Colombo 🍽️ Breakfast & Clubhouse Lunch 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 03

Transfer to Victoria Golf Resort Kandy

Private luxury transfer into the central hills. Afternoon practice round on Victoria's undulating greens.

🏨 Overnight: Victoria Golf Resort, Kandy 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 04

Round 2: 18 Holes at Victoria Championship Course

First competitive 18 holes on Donald Steel’s spectacular lake-bordered fairways.

🏨 Overnight: Victoria Golf Resort, Kandy 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 05

Round 3: 18 Holes Victoria Re-Match & Temple Visit

Second full 18-hole round at Victoria. Evening private cultural blessing at the Temple of the Tooth.

🏨 Overnight: Victoria Golf Resort, Kandy 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 06

Scenic Ascent to Nuwara Eliya Alpine Highlands

Drive past Devon Falls to Nuwara Eliya. Check into the historic Grand Hotel.

🏨 Overnight: The Grand Hotel, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 07

Round 4: 18 Holes at Nuwara Eliya Golf Club (NEGC)

Tee off amidst crisp mountain air and towering pines at the 135-year-old NEGC course.

🏨 Overnight: The Grand Hotel, Nuwara Eliya 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 08

Scenic Descent to Shangri-La Hambantota Golf Links

Scenic highway drive to the southern coast. Relax in oceanfront resort pools.

🏨 Overnight: Shangri-La Golf Resort, Hambantota 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 09

Round 5: 18 Holes at Shangri-La Coastal Links

Play Rodney Wright's coastal course through former sapphire mines and coastal lagoons.

🏨 Overnight: Shangri-La Golf Resort, Hambantota 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 10

Round 6: 18 Holes Championship Finale at Shangri-La

Final 18-hole championship finale round with trophy presentation dinner.

🏨 Overnight: Shangri-La Golf Resort, Hambantota 🍽️ Breakfast & Awards Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 11

Coastal Transfer to Galle Fort & Lighthouse Walk

Drive to Galle. Relax with luxury spa massages and sunset stroll along the ramparts.

🏨 Overnight: Historic Fort Hotel, Galle 🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
Day 12

Express Highway Transfer to Colombo Airport & Departure

Private chauffeur transfer directly to CMB airport for onward journey.

🏨 Overnight: Departure 🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗 Approx. 2.5 - 3.5 Hours (110 km)
