Cancellation & Payment Policy

Transparent terms designed with fairness, flexibility, and guest peace of mind.

Flexible Postponement Assurance: We recognize that travel plans can be disrupted by unforeseen circumstances. Where permissible by hotel partners, Lotus Ceylon offers flexible rescheduling of dates.

1. Booking Deposit

To confirm your bespoke itinerary and secure your chauffeur allocation and boutique accommodations, a deposit of 20% to 30% of the total tour value is required. The specific deposit amount will be stated on your formal booking quotation.

2. Balance Payment

The remaining balance is due 30 days prior to your arrival in Sri Lanka. For reservations finalized within 30 days of arrival, full payment is required upon confirmation.

3. Standard Cancellation Schedule

Should you need to cancel your confirmed journey, notification must be submitted in writing via email. The following standard cancellation charges apply:

  • 45 days or more prior to arrival: Deposit refunded less unavoidable bank processing fees and pre-issued non-refundable permits.
  • 30 to 44 days prior to arrival: 25% of total tour cost retained.
  • 15 to 29 days prior to arrival: 50% of total tour cost retained.
  • Under 14 days prior to arrival or No-Show: 100% of total tour cost retained.

4. Peak Season & Non-Refundable Items

Reservations falling within the festive peak season (December 20 – January 10) or during the historic Kandy Esala Perahera festival are subject to strict non-refundable terms imposed by boutique properties. Similarly, pre-purchased first-class scenic train tickets and national park safari permits are non-refundable once issued by government departments.

5. Rescheduling Policy

If you need to postpone your journey due to medical emergencies or flight cancellations, we will coordinate with all partner properties to transfer your funds to new travel dates within a 12-month period, subject to seasonal rate adjustments.
