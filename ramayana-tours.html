Discover the Sacred Ramayana Trail Circuits: Divine Sanctuaries, Mythological Legends & Holy Shrines

Follow the ancient epic of the Ramayana across Sri Lanka’s most revered pilgrimage sites. Pay homage at the legendary Munneswaram and Manavari Shiva temples, explore the mountain gardens of Ashoka Vatika and Seetha Amman Temple, visit the mysterious Ravana Cave and waterfalls in Ella, and witness the sacred site of Divurumpola where Sita Devi proved her devotion through the Agni Pariksha.

Signature Ramayana Circuit:
Chilaw (Munneswaram) ➔ Kandy ➔ Nuwara Eliya (Ashoka Vatika & Seetha Amman) ➔ Ella (Ravana Falls) ➔ Divurumpola ➔ Kelaniya Temple
